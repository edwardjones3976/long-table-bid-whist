## Grand product rebaseline — committee conclusion

Status: **RESHAPED**

The frozen WebXR candidate remains useful evidence and a rules/interaction laboratory, but it is no longer the product ceiling.

### Committee record

- Claude: **C** — narrow staged hybrid, with native Quest as the only flagship.
- Grok: **A** — native only; strongest dissent against three unfinished products.
- Gemini/Antigravity: **C** — native flagship plus gated discovery/companion surfaces.
- Cursor: **UNAVAILABLE** — three bounded CLI routes returned no attributable final position; the Chrome fallback required unconfigured Cloud Agents. No vote was fabricated.
- OpenAI participant: **ABSTAIN** — moderated and synthesized but did not freeze an independent position before exposure.

The advisory tally favored C but did not reach a majority of the fixed denominator. The Director therefore issued an evidence-based ruling rather than claiming consensus.

### Ruling: C-narrow

1. The native Quest app is the sole flagship and sole authoritative game.
2. The existing WebXR build freezes as a reference/kernel harness; any later companion may receive public projections only and may contain no gameplay adjudication or concealed state.
3. A Horizon World is not approved as a second card game. After a successful private-game-night alpha, a bounded spike may test discovery, event, identity, and deep-link value only.
4. If companions displace native milestones, duplicate rules, receive concealed data, split matchmaking, or create a second host workflow, the direction automatically collapses to native-only A.

### New product promise

> A native Quest flagship that feels like a living social world: arrive as yourself, sit at a believable table, and play Bid Whist with people you care about using your hands or controllers.

The next three stages are **The First Deal**, **Private Game Night Alpha**, and **Long Table House Beta**. No native implementation, physical Quest proof, avatar integration, multiplayer service, cultural acceptance, rights clearance, merge, or owner acceptance is implied by this committee milestone.
