## Protocol

1. OpenAI freezes the evidence, question, options, and criteria.
2. Claude, Cursor, Grok, and invited Antigravity provide independent positions without seeing one another's answers.
3. OpenAI creates a neutral cross-position digest containing every material disagreement.
4. Each available provider receives one focused rebuttal round.
5. OpenAI records the weighted advisory result, preserves dissent, runs a counter-bias check, and issues a `RESHAPED` recommendation. The vote grants no implementation, merge, deployment, publication, purchase, privacy, or acceptance authority.

Perplexity and Firecrawl supply evidence and do not vote. Planning/debate is overall effort and earns no direct-production workload credit.

## Rebuttal rule

The rebuttal packet will contain a bounded neutral summary of positions, not raw private provider transcripts. Each provider must challenge the strongest opposing option and name any evidence that would change its vote. No provider gets a second revision after rebuttal unless genuinely new evidence appears.
