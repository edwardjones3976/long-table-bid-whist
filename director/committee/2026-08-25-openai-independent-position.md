# Independent position — OpenAI participant

- **Committee:** `LT-COM-2026-08-25-REENTRY`
- **Formed independently:** yes
- **Advisory vote:** `RESHAPE`

## Position

1. **Milestone direction:** make the first credible milestone a paired proof:
   (a) a deterministic browser rules/interaction laboratory and (b) a narrow
   Quest-native seated-room spike proving scale, comfort, Meta avatars, robot
   dealer presence, and controller/hand reach. Neither alone is the MVP.
2. **Rejected alternative:** a polished browser room presented as a Quest
   prototype. It is fast and useful, but it cannot establish headset comfort,
   SDK integration, embodied interaction, multiplayer privacy, spatial voice,
   or performance.
3. **In scope:** one named rules manifest, one full seeded hand, four seats,
   AI Dealer/Human Host selection, legal-information AI, replayable actions,
   accessibility baseline, private-room state model, and one Quest room slice.
   **Deferred:** public matchmaking, progression, commerce, multiple rooms,
   shared host music, tournaments, spectators, and production networking.
4. **Authority:** the game service alone owns deal, legal actions, trick and
   score. The robot dealer explains and advances legal flow. The Human Host
   owns invites, seats, mute/remove, pause requests, accessibility help, and
   between-hand substitution; never hands, bids, tricks, or score.
5. **Experience decisions:** seated/no locomotion; controller-complete with
   hand parity; user Meta avatars only; captions and independent audio levels;
   native mute/block/leave/report; warm room and table behavior instead of
   stereotyped cultural props. Music off by default; no bundled track without
   composition-and-recording provenance.
6. **Proof order/tools:** rules manifest and reducer → invariant tests →
   browser task flow → cultural/accessibility critique → Quest-native room and
   input spike → authoritative networking/reconnection design → multiplayer.
7. **Acceptance gates:** card conservation and deterministic replay; no hidden
   information leakage; successful full-hand task flow; seated/readability and
   controller/hand evidence on Quest; experienced-player plus cultural and
   accessibility human critique with no serious stop.
8. **Top risks:** treating a browser mock as headset evidence; house-rule or AI
   errors that experienced players reject; cultural/music choices that imply
   authenticity or clearance without human/provenance evidence.

The premature OpenAI-authored room draft is evidence of speed only. It should
be quarantined from direct-work accounting and may be reused only after the
committee assigns a provider-owned candidate that independently satisfies the
conclusion.
