# Claude and Gemini Chrome advisory review

## Why Chrome was used

Two authenticated Claude CLI review attempts ended at their explicit cost
ceilings without a terminal verdict. They receive no review credit. Under the
owner's fallback instruction, the browser candidate was then presented in
Claude and Gemini through authenticated Chrome sessions.

The Chrome reviewers received a bounded evidence summary, not repository or
concealed-card access. Their results are advisory browser-proof critiques, not
exact-source reviews and not substitutes for Antigravity.

## Verdicts

- Claude in Chrome: `CHANGES_REQUIRED_BROWSER_PROOF`
- Gemini in Chrome: `CHANGES_REQUIRED_BROWSER_PROOF`

## Convergent findings used

1. Closed drawers needed to leave neither controls nor tab stops behind.
2. Leave during an active hand needed confirm/cancel.
3. Block needed an observable local effect and explicit no-network/no-game-state
   copy.
4. Report needed to say it was recorded locally and was not filed or sent.
5. The disabled pause control needed a visible and programmatic reason.
6. Remove-to-AI needed a visible West-seat handoff.
7. A real Chrome walkthrough, screenshot, and keyboard path were still missing.

## Findings retained for later gates

- A rendered Host boundary is not an enforceable authority boundary. A
  session/core provider must prove that the host never receives concealed
  hands and cannot mutate rules-owned state.
- Withholding level 7 in the UI is not proof that the engine rejects an
  unreviewed level-7 action.
- Browser tests are not Quest/OpenXR, Meta Avatar SDK, controller, hand-input,
  device-performance, or human usability evidence.
- Cultural fit for the intended 30–70-year-old foundational Black American
  audience remains a human research and test question, not a model verdict.
- Local prototype block/report affordances are not a production moderation
  service and must not be described as one.

## Director disposition

The UI-owned findings were returned to Cursor and independently corrected by
OpenAI where the second Cursor candidate remained incomplete. The deeper
authority, rules, Quest, avatar, and human-fit findings stay open. Neither
browser reviewer approved implementation, merge, publication, or completion.
