## Frozen decision question

What direction, scope, product boundaries, technical proof sequence, toolchain,
and acceptance gates should govern the first credible milestone for a seated
social VR/MR Bid Whist experience on Meta Quest for adults 30–70?

The answer must preserve:

1. user-created Meta avatars for all human players;
2. a clearly non-human robot as the optional AI Dealer;
3. an optional Human Host with event powers but no concealed-information or outcome authority;
4. light Foundational Black American warmth expressed through hospitality,
   room energy, materials, pacing, and table culture—not caricature or added
   demographic characters;
5. a rights-clean bundled-audio policy and an explicitly separate policy for
   host-controlled external/private music;
6. a serious, deterministic Bid Whist rules core with named rules variants;
7. seated comfort, controller and hand access, readable cards, captions, and
   social safety for a broad 30–70-year-old audience;
8. honest distinction between a browser interaction proof and Quest-native,
   networking, privacy, moderation, avatar, and comfort evidence.
