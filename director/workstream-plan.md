## D1-D3 assignment packet

- **Starting state:** The concept, critique, private repository, branch, worktree,
  coordination surface, and zero-dollar phase budget are established.
- **Target outcome:** One bounded prototype specification with explicit state,
  interaction, host/dealer, and rights requirements.
- **Scope:** Project documents in the confirmed private repository; no mutation
  of `sources/`.
- **Provider:** OpenAI Director for framing and synthesis.
- **Authority granted:** Create and revise project-local planning artifacts.
- **Authority denied:** Paid provider launches, Slack posts, uploads, deployment,
  publication, purchases, credential changes, or licensing commitments.
- **Acceptance checks:** Deterministic rule transitions; clear card-commit UX;
  server-authoritative concealed state; complete dealer/host permission matrix;
  rights-clean audio paths; accessibility coverage.
- **Evidence required:** Scenario tables, state transitions, role matrix, and
  explicitly marked unknowns.
- **Bound:** One strict preset, one room, four seats, optional AI fill, VR plus
  only the MR interactions needed to place and use the same table.
- **Fallback:** Select the smallest local toolchain at G1 instead of exploring
  engines or providers broadly.
- **Handoff target:** Director technical selection at G1, then a bounded producer.

`BOOTSTRAP` -> `PLAN` -> `WORKING` / `EVIDENCE` -> `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED`
-> future `REVIEW_READY` -> `COMPLETE`, `PAUSED`, or `RESHAPED`.

   local provider assignment.

   evidence.

  prototype specification; repository, branch, worktree, and $0 provider budget
  are already confirmed.

- Rights review before shared host-supplied music or archival/cultural assets.
- Different-provider exact-candidate review before merge or release.
- Current CI, security/privacy, and asset-provenance evidence when applicable.
