## Non-overlapping ownership

- Claude: no active foundation lease. Its terminal foundation bytes remain
  provenance-frozen, and future XR-test/RobotDealer leases open only after the
  active Grok fallback is OpenAI-accepted.
- Cursor: `Unreal/Plugins/LongTableXR/**` only. Input, Presence, Environment,
  source, tests and any plugin-local configuration remain inside that subtree.
- Grok: while `LT-NATIVE-GROK-AUTHORITY-SAFETY-001` is active, only its seven
  exact table/WebSocket/Unreal-client code and regression paths. Later packets
  may separately own `services/session-gateway`, `Unreal/Source/LongTableSocial`,
  `Unreal/Content/Social`, or clean-room `Unreal/Plugins/LongTableHouseCore`.
- Antigravity: no active path. Its rejected `LongTableHouseCore` and
  `LongTableRobotDealer` candidates, sandboxes, frozen QA copies, transcripts,
  hashes and diffs remain quarantined historical evidence and are never inputs
  to a fallback lane.
- OpenAI: read-only QA of candidate paths plus writes only under
  `director/native-program` and `artifacts/native-program` for findings,
  packets and evidence. Any code correction is kicked back to its owner.

The shared integration contract is frozen before dispatch. Providers may add
tests only within their owned module. They may not alter another provider's
header, schema, fixture, Build.cs, Blueprint, service or test. Integration
failures are findings, not permission to cross-write.

For Claude R1, the one-writer lease also carries forward the exact engine and
auxiliary paths listed in `LT-NATIVE-CLAUDE-001-R1.json`: `Unreal/LongTable.uproject`,
`Unreal/Config/**`, the two direct `Unreal/Source/*.Target.cs` files,
`fixtures/protocol/**`, `scripts/native/**`, and `.github/workflows/native-ci.yml`.
No later provider may edit those accepted dependencies.

Claude foundation-final is terminal at exact candidate
`a6742a04a384a88e7342530041a1f0d3ab71db2c`: executable TypeScript and Windows
build gates passed, but OpenAI rejected coalesced post-close frame dispatch,
multi-listener Unreal reentrancy and unbounded intent-ledger memory. It earns
medium/5 overall-effort points and zero direct-production points. The only
active foundation writer lease is now `LT-NATIVE-GROK-AUTHORITY-SAFETY-001`,
limited to seven exact paths and one primary dispatch.

## Sequential exhausted-route fallback leases

These are new assignments, not extra revisions or transport retries. They may
never overlap one another.

1. `LT-NATIVE-GROK-AUTHORITY-SAFETY-001` transfers seven exact defect and test
   paths from terminal Claude to under-target Grok, starting from `a6742a0`.
   It may not edit protocol, fixtures, XR, RobotDealer, social or control paths.
2. After that fallback is OpenAI-accepted, `LT-NATIVE-CLAUDE-XRTEST-001`
   transfers from terminal Cursor to Claude only
   `Unreal/Plugins/LongTableXR/Source/LongTableXR/Private/Tests/LongTableXRTests.cpp`
   at exact base `33aca6106603342efcf43fb813482cb18fd4b18c`. The rest of
   that exact XR plugin is a readable immutable dependency. No production XR
   decoder or other file is writable.
3. After both the foundation fallback and XR packets are frozen and OpenAI-QA terminal, assignment
   `LT-NATIVE-CLAUDE-ROBOT-001` transfers the clean-room destination
   `Unreal/Plugins/LongTableRobotDealer/**` from terminal Antigravity to Claude.
   Claude may read only the new frozen public contract, its own accepted public
   presentation interfaces if available, and UE5.7 public headers. It may not
   read or compare any Antigravity implementation or evidence.

Each lease ends when its exact provider result becomes terminal. OpenAI may
freeze, build, test, accept or reject the bytes, but cannot correct them.
