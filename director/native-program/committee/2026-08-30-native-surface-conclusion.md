# Native surface committee conclusion

## Decision

Build **The Long Table as a standalone native Unreal Engine 5.7.4 Quest app**.
Do not make a Horizon world, companion surface or hybrid identity layer part of
First Deal. Those options remain future experiments only after the native table
passes unchanged-package device, two-headset, comfort and human acceptance.

## Evidence considered

- Firecrawl's ten-source official Meta/Epic matrix:
  `artifacts/native-program/research/2026-08-30-quest-platform-matrix.md`.
- Grok's independent public-only dissent, exact canonical response digest
  `sha256:be6c0cfb5290aa23f2c683bde52898999a93ad7eb67baa2f8b6f55ec6883b8ba`.
- The Director workload, roles and accepted Stage 1 browser oracle.
- Historical Claude/Cursor/Antigravity implementation evidence, used only for
  capability and failure analysis—not as fresh committee votes or accepted code.

## Product rules now frozen

1. **Hands and controllers are equal.** One typed intent grammar serves both.
   Switching cancels transient selection/grab/preview state before reacquiring;
   tracking loss fails closed without duplicating a card or commitment.
2. **VR is complete; MR is optional.** Passthrough, room data and anchors can
   dress the same table, but their denial, loss or instability returns cleanly to
   the seated VR salon without invalidating the hand.
3. **Dealer and host are separate authority roles.** A visibly robotic AI dealer
   handles presentation and cadence. A human event host gets only explicit
   field-by-field table operations such as pause, reseat, mute and start/stop;
   neither can alter rules, concealed cards or committed play.
4. **Identity has a deterministic fallback.** The current official Unreal
   evidence does not support promising the individual's Meta avatar. First Deal
   uses tracked hands/controllers, display name, seat marker and simple presence.
   A future supported avatar adapter may replace the presentation, never the game.
5. **Culture is atmospheric, not performative.** Hospitality, warmth, patience,
   dignity and lived-in table energy may lightly reflect Foundational Black
   American spirit. Dialect imitation, caricature, costume shorthand and claims
   to represent all FBA people or all ages 30–70 are rejected.
6. **Music fails closed.** Only original, commissioned, public-domain or otherwise
   documented-cleared compositions and recordings ship. Host music stays outside
   the app and is never ingested, cached, streamed, recorded, fingerprinted or
   represented as cleared.
7. **First Deal stays small but impressive.** One seated table, one complete deal,
   controller/hand parity, AI dealer/human host option, clean VR presentation,
   readable accessibility defaults and deterministic authority are the spectacle.
   Social scale, persistent anchors, custom avatars, hybrid surfaces and DJ modes
   are deferred.

## Dissent retained

Horizon-first has a real advantage in existing identity and event distribution.
It loses this decision because it conflicts with the native Unreal requirement,
does not establish the same input and audio-control boundaries from the supplied
evidence, and would create another unverified surface before one table works on
device. Revisit only if the native requirement is explicitly dropped or native
device tests falsify the approach.

## Implementation boundary

This conclusion does not widen the current foundation repair. Claude sequence 16
still owns only the 17 authority/identity paths in its immutable packet. XR,
presentation, avatar fallback, MR, host UX and audio work remain separate future
leases after the foundation passes OpenAI exact-candidate QA.
