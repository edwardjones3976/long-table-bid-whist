# STRICT_TABLE_V1 — DRAFT (Rev. 2 — full replacement, not errata)

Status: DRAFT — not ratified. Produced as a bounded provider deliverable from current engine evidence and stated project boundaries. No human-player approval is implied or claimed. Nothing in this document resolves any HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item; provisional values exist only to keep a prototype runnable, and this revision *removes* several values that Rev. 1 wrongly promoted to invariant status.

**Revision note:** This is a full replacement of Rev. 1, correcting three independent-review findings: (1) deck/deal shape was silently promoted to PRODUCT_INVARIANTS despite being a named council question — reclassified to PROVISIONAL_HOUSE_RULES; (2) auction turn-order specifics (one action per seat, left-of-dealer opening, clockwise rotation) were silently promoted to invariant status despite passer re-entry and auction convention being open — reclassified to PROVISIONAL_HOUSE_RULES; (3) the authority model implied a live server/session Authoritative Service exists — it does not; the current artifact is a local single-process TypeScript prototype, added as a new Critical discrepancy. All cross-references and conformance vectors below have been updated accordingly.

## 0. Scope and Authority Model

The following describes the **target** architecture this manifest is written for. It is not a description of the current implementation — see DISC-1 below, which states plainly that this authority boundary does not yet exist as a server/session construct.

- The Authoritative Service (AS) is intended to be the sole owner of shuffle, deal, legal-action computation, turn order, trick resolution, scoring, recovery, and rules enforcement. No client, including the Human Host client, is meant to compute or assert any of these once the AS exists.
- Human Host is intended to hold session/social authority only: invite, seat/unseat between hands, start/pause-at-summary, kick/mute/report, and (if HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED items resolve it) host succession. Human Host is meant to have zero visibility into concealed hands, kitty contents pre-reveal, or any AS internal state, and zero ability to mutate committed cards, bids, tricks, score, or rules.
- AI seats are meant to receive only: (a) their own hand as dealt/updated, and (b) public history (bids made, cards led/played, contract, trick winners, running score). AI seats must never receive the root seed, the deck order, or any opponent's concealed cards.
- Human seats are represented by each participant's Meta avatar in the target runtime; the AI Dealer is rendered as an unmistakable robot, never a human or culturally-coded figure.

**As of this draft, none of the four bullets above are enforced by a server or session boundary.** The current codebase is a local, single-process TypeScript prototype in which "Host cannot see hands" and "AI receives only its own hand" are, at best, object-visibility conventions inside one process — not an access-control boundary that a client cannot bypass. Treat every AS-authority claim in this manifest as a target requirement, not a verified current property, until DISC-1 is closed.

## 1. PRODUCT_INVARIANTS

These are non-negotiable regardless of table tradition. Rev. 2 deliberately narrows this section: anything that encodes a *specific* tradition-dependent value (card count, cards per seat, kitty size, whose turn opens, rotation direction, action-repeat rules) has been moved to Section 2, because deck/deal tradition and auction convention are both named HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED items. What remains here is *structural* — true regardless of which tradition the council eventually picks.

- **INV-1 Seat/Team Topology.** Exactly four seats: South, West, North, East. Fixed partnerships: North–South vs East–West. *(Not a named council item; retained as structural.)*
- **INV-2 Deck/Deal Shape Must Be Declared and Complete.** Exactly one versioned deck/deal shape must be declared by the manifest in force at runtime, and that shape must fully account for every card in play — no card may be undefined, duplicated, or unaccounted for between deck, hands, and kitty. This invariant does **not** fix the card count, cards-per-seat, or kitty size; those are tradition-dependent and are PROVISIONAL (Section 2) pending HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item 1.
- **INV-3 Hidden Information Boundary.** No seat (human or AI) and no non-authoritative party may ever receive another seat's concealed cards or the root seed. This includes debug/telemetry paths. *(Structural privacy guarantee — independent of which deal tradition is chosen.)*
- **INV-4 Host Non-Authority.** Human Host cannot inspect hands, kitty contents pre-award, or bids-in-progress beyond what all seats publicly see; cannot mutate any committed game state. Host powers are enumerated in Section 0 and may not be silently expanded.
- **INV-5 Cultural Presentation.** Audience direction (30–70-year-old foundational Black American players) informs environmental warmth and hospitality tone only. No bodies, dialect, costume, stereotype, or invented demographic characters may be encoded into rules, copy, or assets governed by this manifest.
- **INV-6 AI Dealer Identity.** The AI Dealer must be presented as unmistakably non-human (robot), never as a stand-in human character.
- **INV-7 Music/Audio.** Ships Off by default; no bundled or recorded audio. Any host playback is external, private-room, unrecorded, and rights-dependent, and is out of scope for this rules manifest.
- **INV-8 Deterministic Replay Requires Authorized Access Only.** The engine may be internally deterministic for testing/recovery, but that determinism must never be exposed to any client-facing surface (PlayerView, digest, logs shipped to clients).
- **INV-9 Turn/Order Determinism (structural only).** Whatever auction and play order convention the council adopts, it must be explicit, deterministic, and enforced by the AS such that each seat's legal action is accepted exactly once per turn under that convention. This invariant does **not** fix opening seat, rotation direction, or whether a passed seat may re-enter; those are tradition-dependent and are PROVISIONAL (Section 2) pending HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED items 6, 7, and 8.

## 2. PROVISIONAL_HOUSE_RULES

Each entry is a temporary value needed to keep a runnable prototype moving. Each carries an explicit rationale and is flagged for override the moment the matching HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item resolves. None of these may be treated as settled tradition, and none may be cited as satisfying INV-2 or INV-9's *structural* requirement that a shape/order be declared — they are only today's placeholder for that declaration.

- **PROV-1 Deck Composition (reclassified from Rev. 1 INV-2).** 54 cards: standard 52 plus Little Joker and Big Joker. *Rationale:* matches current engine behavior; deck/deal tradition is HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item 1 and this specific count (vs. e.g. a smaller pack, or Jokers-out variants) is not yet council-confirmed.
- **PROV-2 Deal Shape (reclassified from Rev. 1 INV-3).** 12 cards per seat (48 total), 6-card kitty. *Rationale:* matches current engine behavior and satisfies INV-2's completeness requirement (54 = 48 + 6) for now, but the specific split is exactly what HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item 1 is expected to weigh in on.
- **PROV-3 Auction Turn/Action Rule (reclassified from Rev. 1 INV-10).** Left-of-dealer seat opens the auction; turn order proceeds clockwise; each seat gets exactly one auction action per turn with no re-entry after a pass. *Rationale:* matches current engine and satisfies INV-9's structural requirement for now, but passer re-entry (council item 6) and auction convention generally (council item 8, and adjacent to item 7) are explicitly unresolved — this is one plausible convention among several, not doctrine.
- **PROV-4 Dealer Default & Rotation.** Dealer defaults to East for the first hand; dealer rotates seat-by-seat (clockwise) each subsequent hand. *Rationale:* matches current engine behavior; no council guidance yet on traditional dealer selection (e.g., cut-for-deal) or rotation direction.
- **PROV-5 Bid Range & Mode Ordering.** Bids run 3–7. At the same numeric level, modes rank Low < High < No-Trump Low < No-Trump High. *Rationale:* matches current engine; bid-7/Boston meaning and same-level precedence are explicitly open council questions (items 8, 10), so this ordering is a placeholder, not doctrine.
- **PROV-6 Bid 7 Target-Disabled (target state — see DISC-2 for current non-compliance).** The intended rule is that level 7 is not selectable in the UI and is rejected by the AS if attempted programmatically. *Rationale:* current scoring formula (PROV-13) makes 7 mathematically impossible to fulfill under 12 tricks; shipping a selectable 7 before Boston/overtrick rules are resolved would let players commit to an unwinnable contract. **This rule is not yet met by the current codebase — see DISC-2: `legalBids` currently exposes level 7.**
- **PROV-7 Forced Dealer Cannot Pass.** If bidding reaches the dealer with all other seats having passed, the dealer must bid (cannot pass). *Rationale:* matches current engine; this is one plausible "someone must buy the hand" convention among several regional variants, and is explicitly HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED item 7.
- **PROV-8 Trump Declaration & Kitty Handling.** Auction winner declares a suit for High/Low contracts or declares "no suit" for No-Trump; winner receives the kitty, discards exactly 6 cards face-down to reach a 12-card playing hand, then leads first. *Rationale:* matches current engine; discard-as-book status (council item 2) and kitty/discard visibility to opponents are open, so discards are treated as fully concealed (never revealed to any seat) pending resolution. Depends on PROV-2's kitty size, not fixed independently.
- **PROV-9 Suited-Contract Joker Trump.** In High/Low (suited) contracts, both Jokers become trump; Big Joker outranks Little Joker, both outrank all cards of the trump suit. *Rationale:* matches current engine and is the least contested part of Joker handling; still provisional because complete Joker order/lead/follow-suit behavior is council item 11.
- **PROV-10 Low-Contract Rank Reversal (Ordinary Cards Only).** In Low contracts, ordinary-suit rank direction reverses (e.g., 2 beats King) for follow/trick-winner comparisons among non-Joker cards. *Rationale:* matches current engine. Joker treatment under Low is explicitly marked provisional in engine evidence and is NOT extended by this manifest — see DISC-4; Low-contract Joker rank is unresolved, not merely provisional.
- **PROV-11 Follow-Suit Enforcement.** A seat must follow the suit led if able; if unable, any card (including trump) may be played. *Rationale:* standard trick-taking mechanic present in current engine; retained as provisional pending council confirmation of table vocabulary/exceptions (council item 4; ties into revoke ruling, item 12).
- **PROV-12 Twelve Tricks Per Hand.** Exactly 12 tricks are played per hand, one per remaining card in each 12-card post-discard hand. *Rationale:* arithmetic consequence of PROV-2 and PROV-8, not itself independently contested — but it is now doubly provisional, since it cascades from PROV-2's deal shape, which is itself pending council item 1. This is the fact that breaks the bid-7 scoring formula (see DISC-2).
- **PROV-13 Scoring Formula (Interim, Non-Authoritative for Bid 7).** Declaring team scores +bid_level if tricks won ≥ 6 + bid_level; otherwise scores −bid_level. *Rationale:* matches current engine for bids 3–6 only; explicitly does NOT extend to bid 7 (target-disabled per PROV-6) and does not define overtrick, defender-point, or match-end scoring — all open council items (item 13). This formula is a placeholder for prototype scorekeeping, not a proposal for final scoring.
- **PROV-14 No Misdeal / No Redeal Handling Yet.** The prototype does not implement misdeal detection, void-hand redeal, or timeout/disconnect-triggered redeal; any malformed deal is currently just impossible by construction (deck is engine-shuffled). *Rationale:* deck/deal tradition and timeout/disconnect/void/redeal (council items 1, 14) are both named council items; the prototype avoids the question by construction rather than answering it.

## 3. HUMAN_COUNCIL_REQUIRED

No PRODUCT_INVARIANT or PROVISIONAL_HOUSE_RULE resolves any of these. Each is listed with the provisional stand-in (if any) currently blocking it from being a silent decision.

1. Deck/deal tradition and misdeal handling — stand-ins: PROV-1 (54-card composition), PROV-2 (12/seat + 6-kitty split), PROV-14 (misdeal avoided by construction, not handled). *Explicitly no longer treated as settled by any invariant — see INV-2.*
2. Whether the six-card discard counts as a "book" — stand-in: PROV-8 (discard fully concealed, book status unaddressed).
3. Joker discard/reveal rules — unaddressed; no stand-in.
4. Table vocabulary (naming of bids, contracts, calls) — unaddressed; engine uses internal identifiers only.
5. Exact bid meanings (what each level/mode commits a team to beyond raw trick count) — stand-in: PROV-5/PROV-13, both explicitly partial.
6. Passer re-entry (can a passed seat bid again later in the same auction) — stand-in: PROV-3 (no re-entry path in current engine). *Explicitly no longer treated as settled by any invariant — see INV-9.*
7. Forced-dealer convention — stand-in: PROV-7.
8. Same-level mode precedence — stand-in: PROV-5.
9. No-Trump variants (High/Low distinction meaning, Joker suit behavior) — stand-in: none. Currently a **defect with active non-compliant behavior**, not a rule — see DISC-5.
10. Bid 7 / Boston definition and scoring — stand-in: PROV-6 (target-disabled). **Current codebase does not meet this target — see DISC-2.**
11. Complete Joker order and lead/follow-suit behavior — stand-in: PROV-9 (suited only); PROV-10 explicitly excludes Joker behavior in Low.
12. Revoke (renege) ruling — unaddressed; ties to PROV-11.
13. Scoring: overtricks, defender points, match end condition — stand-in: PROV-13 (declaring-team-only, no overtrick/defender/match-end logic) — see DISC-7.
14. Timeout/disconnect/void/redeal — unaddressed; see PROV-14.
15. Substitution restoration (mid-match seat replacement rules) — unaddressed at rules level; AS is asserted as *target* owner of "substitution" per Section 0 but the restoration policy itself is undefined, and the AS itself does not yet exist as a boundary — see DISC-1.
16. Host succession (what happens if the Human Host leaves/disconnects) — unaddressed; Human Host powers (Section 0) exist only as intent, not as an enforced boundary — see DISC-1.

## 5. CONFORMANCE_VECTOR_CATALOG

Each vector names a scenario, the layer it exercises, and the pass condition. All vectors assume the **target** AS architecture is the source of truth. Per DISC-1, that AS boundary does not yet exist in the current codebase — vectors below are written as target acceptance tests for the AS once built, and each notes explicitly where it is currently unenforceable or currently failing against today's single-process prototype, so this catalog cannot be quietly read as "already passing."

1. **CV-DEAL-COUNT-01** — Deck/Deal Shape Completeness. Deal a fresh hand under the PROV-1/PROV-2 provisional shape (54-card deck, 12 per seat, 6-card kitty); assert the shape is internally complete (48 + 6 = 54, no duplicates) per INV-2's structural requirement. *Pass:* counts match PROV-1/PROV-2 exactly with zero unaccounted cards. *Note:* this vector validates internal consistency of whichever shape is declared — it does not and cannot validate that 54/12/6 is the "correct" tradition, since that is council item 1.
2. **CV-DEALER-FORCED-01** — Forced Dealer. Drive an auction where South, West, North all pass in turn with East as dealer (PROV-4 default); assert East cannot submit a pass action and the rule engine rejects a pass attempt from East, per PROV-7. *Pass:* pass is rejected; East's only legal actions are non-pass bids. *Note:* currently testable only against the in-process engine (DISC-1) — not yet a true AS-boundary test.
3. **CV-BID-ORDER-01** — Bid Ordering. At a fixed numeric level, submit mode selections in each pairing (Low vs High, High vs NT-Low, NT-Low vs NT-High) and assert the legal-action set only allows a strictly higher mode at the same level, per PROV-5's Low < High < No-Trump Low < No-Trump High ordering. *Pass:* illegal same-or-lower mode/level submissions are rejected.
4. **CV-BID7-REJECT-01** — Level-7 Rejection (Target, Currently Failing). Attempt to submit a bid of 7 both via UI action and a direct rule-engine call bypassing the UI. *Target pass condition:* both surfaces reject it, honoring PROV-6/DISC-2. **Current status: FAILS at the rule-engine layer** — `legalBids` currently includes level 7, so a UI-bypassing client can reach it today. This vector should be run as a regression check and is expected to go from FAIL to PASS once DISC-2's implementation-level correction lands.
5. **CV-KITTY-DISCARD-01** — Kitty Award and Discard. Complete an auction; assert the winning seat and only the winning seat receives the 6-card kitty into their hand (now 18 cards, per PROV-2/PROV-8), then must discard exactly 6 to return to 12, and discarded cards become invisible to all other seats and to Human Host. *Pass:* hand sizes are exactly 18 then 12; discard pile is not present in any other seat's or the Host's view. *Note:* the "invisible to Host" half of this pass condition is only meaningfully testable once DISC-1's process boundary exists; today it can only confirm the object isn't rendered in the Host UI, not that the Host client cannot access it.
6. **CV-JOKER-SUITED-01** — Suited-Contract Joker Trump. In a High or Low (suited) contract, assert both Jokers are legal trump plays in any trick, and in a trick containing both Jokers, Big Joker is ruled the winner over Little Joker regardless of other cards played. *Pass:* trick-winner computation matches PROV-9 ordering exactly.
7. **CV-LOW-RANK-01** — Low-Contract Rank Reversal. In a Low contract, construct a trick with only ordinary (non-Joker) cards of the same suit at varying ranks; assert the lowest ordinary rank wins, not the highest. *Pass:* trick-winner logic reverses ordinary rank order under Low per PROV-10, and the vector explicitly excludes Jokers (Joker-in-Low is out of scope pending council resolution, per DISC-4).
8. **CV-NT-JOKER-REJECT-01** — No-Trump Joker Unresolved Rejection (Target, Currently Failing). Attempt to start or continue a No-Trump contract while the No-Trump Joker rule remains unresolved. *Target pass condition:* the rule engine refuses to enter/continue play under No-Trump rather than falling back to an undefined "joker suit" behavior. **Current status: FAILS** — per DISC-5, the current engine/UI currently allow No-Trump contracts to be entered and played despite the incomplete Joker rule. This vector should fail loudly today and is the regression check for DISC-5's required correction.
9. **CV-FOLLOWSUIT-01** — Follow Suit Enforcement. With a led suit established and the acting seat holding at least one card of that suit, assert the legal-action set excludes every card not of the led suit. Separately, with the acting seat holding zero cards of the led suit, assert all cards (including trump) are legal. *Pass:* legal-action set matches PROV-11 in both branches.
10. **CV-TRICKWINNER-01** — Trick Winner Determination. Construct a full 4-card trick under a suited (non-Low) contract with one trump card played off-suit; assert the trump card wins regardless of led-suit rank. *Pass:* trick resolution names the trump-card seat as winner.
11. **CV-SCORING-01** — Scoring (Success/Failure Boundary). For a declared bid level N (3 ≤ N ≤ 6 only, per PROV-6's target restriction — see CV-BID7-REJECT-01 for the N=7 case), simulate the declaring team winning exactly 6+N tricks (success) and exactly 6+N−1 tricks (failure) in two separate runs; assert score deltas are +N and −N respectively. *Pass:* scoring matches PROV-13 exactly at both boundary points.
12. **CV-HIDDEN-PROJECTION-01** — Hidden-Information Projection (Target, Currently Failing). For every seat's PlayerView and for the Human Host's view, inspect the full serialized payload; assert no payload contains any other seat's concealed hand, the undealt/discarded kitty cards (pre-reveal), or the root seed, per INV-3. **Current status: FAILS** — per DISC-3 and DISC-4, both PlayerView and publicDigest currently expose the root seed. This vector is the primary regression check once those are fixed, and — per DISC-1 — is only a *true* boundary test once a real client/server split exists; today it can only inspect the in-process object.
13. **CV-DETERMINISTIC-REPLAY-01** — Deterministic Replay (Internal Only, Target). Using an internal/test-only harness (never a client-facing endpoint), replay a hand from a fixed seed twice; assert identical deal, identical legal-action sequences, and identical trick/score outcomes. Separately assert this harness's seed input is not reachable from any client-facing API. *Pass:* replay is bit-identical internally; zero client-facing surface exposes the seed (cross-checked against CV-HIDDEN-PROJECTION-01, which currently fails). *Note:* the "not reachable from any client-facing API" half is meaningless as a boundary test until DISC-1 is resolved — today "client-facing" and "internal" are the same process.
14. **CV-HOST-NONAUTH-01** — Host Non-Authority (Target). As Human Host, attempt each of: reading a concealed hand, reading pre-reveal kitty/discard contents, submitting a bid/play/score-mutation action on behalf of any seat, and altering a committed trick or score value. *Target pass condition:* all four attempts fail, honoring INV-4. **Current status: not a real boundary test** — per DISC-1, there is no enforced client/server separation, so this vector today can only confirm the Host *UI* doesn't expose these actions, not that a Host client is structurally incapable of them. Treat any current "pass" as UI-level only until DISC-1 closes.
15. **CV-SUBSTITUTION-01** — Between-Hand Substitution (Target). Between two hands (never mid-hand), replace one human seat's occupant; assert the new occupant receives a fresh PlayerView with no residual access to the prior occupant's hand history beyond public history, and assert the AS — not the Host — performs the substitution and preserves running score/dealer rotation state. *Pass:* substitution succeeds only via the AS, only between hands, and the new seat's private-data boundary matches INV-3/INV-4 immediately upon entry. *Note:* substitution "restoration" policy itself is council item 15 and unresolved; this vector tests only the boundary/timing mechanics, not the restoration rule. Also gated on DISC-1 — there is currently no AS to perform this as distinct from the Host or client.
