Summary

The prototype is a single-player, in-browser Bid Whist hand against three bots, with a hand-rolled Three.js seated VR table. Roughly 5–10% of the target exists. There is no server, no multiplayer, no Tonk, no Spades, no mansion and no voice. The reusable parts are the Bid Whist rule kernel, the seat-scoped player view, the intent/receipt interaction pattern, and the XR session lifecycle code.
